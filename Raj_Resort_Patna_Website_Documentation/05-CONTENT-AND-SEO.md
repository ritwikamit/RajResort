# Content, Local SEO & Metadata

## Content principles
Use direct, warm hospitality language. Avoid unsupported claims such as “best”, “No. 1”, “five-star”, “award-winning” or “luxury”. Do not copy long booking-site descriptions or publish third-party prices/reviews as fixed facts.

## Title templates
- Home: `Raj Resort Patna | Stay, Rooms & Guest Information`
- Rooms: `Rooms at Raj Resort Patna | Room Options & Amenities`
- Events: `Events & Banquet Enquiries | Raj Resort Patna` (only if confirmed)
- Gallery: `Gallery | Raj Resort Patna`
- Contact: `Contact & Directions | Raj Resort Patna`

Draft meta description for owner approval: “Plan your visit to Raj Resort in Kurji, Patna. View property photos, check stay options, find directions, and continue to your preferred booking provider.”

## Local SEO checklist
Confirm exact business name/address/phone against Google Business Profile; use consistent NAP; verify profile ownership and opening hours; connect Search Console; submit sitemap; use descriptive filenames and accurate alt text; add `Hotel`/`LodgingBusiness` structured data only with confirmed details; do not add rating schema from scraped reviews.

## Analytics
Track booking-provider clicks, phone, WhatsApp, directions and form completion only after owner approval and privacy review. Do not send form contents to analytics.
