### Contact
A Justdial search snapshot displayed 09054394665. Treat this only as a lead; verify phone and WhatsApp with the owner before publication. Email was not verified.

### Rooms
MakeMyTrip currently displays categories including Classic, Deluxe, Standard and Suite. Agoda shows Double Classic, Classic Double or Twin, 2 Bedroom Deluxe and Suite. These third-party labels may not match official inventory. Do not publish room dimensions, occupancy, prices or cancellation policies without confirmation.

### Amenities
Agoda lists Wi-Fi, air conditioning, daily housekeeping, 24-hour front desk and parking. Guest Reservations lists internet access, parking, laundry, room service and 24-hour front desk, but also makes potentially generic/inconsistent claims about restaurant, pool, breakfast and pet-friendliness. MakeMyTrip lists power backup, refrigerator and housekeeping among its amenities. Confirm every amenity before publishing.

### Events and banquets
The supplied Justdial listing/reviews mention wedding functions and banquet use. Search also surfaced https://wedding.rajresortpatna.com/ with wedding-related content. This suggests event positioning but does not prove current services, packages, capacities or availability. Confirm all details with the owner; do not copy awkward/generic text automatically.

### Ratings
A Justdial snapshot showed 4.9 and around 1,470 ratings; MakeMyTrip showed 3.7 based on 38 ratings in the retrieved snapshot. Ratings differ by platform and change. Do not claim one universal rating or import testimonials without approval.
